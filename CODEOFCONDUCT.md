# Lean‑Hallucination‑Guard — Code of Conduct

Lean‑Hallucination‑Guard is a research project built around humanitarian
constraints, geometric clarity, and safe collaboration. This Code of Conduct
exists to ensure that everyone who participates in the project does so in a
way that respects people, protects contributors, and maintains the integrity
of the bounded non‑harm enclave.

This document governs **human behavior**, not technical rules.  
Technical rules live in `LICENSE.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

---

## 🌱 1. Our Principles

All contributors and participants agree to uphold:

- **Respect** — Treat others with dignity, patience, and curiosity.
- **Safety** — Avoid behavior that harms, threatens, or intimidates others.
- **Integrity** — Engage honestly and avoid manipulative or coercive conduct.
- **Humanitarian Alignment** — Support the project’s purpose: preventing harm,
  enabling clarity, and advancing safe geometric‑AI research.

These principles apply to all interactions: issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, and private communication related to the project.

---

## 🛡️ 2. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not allowed:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Attempts to coerce contributors into unsafe or dual‑use development.
- Introducing hostility, trolling, or intentionally derailing conversations.
- Pressuring maintainers to weaken humanitarian constraints or enforcement logic.
- Sharing private information about contributors without consent.
- Using the project’s spaces to promote harmful, violent, or exploitative agendas.

If your behavior undermines the safety or purpose of the project, maintainers
may restrict participation.

---

## 🤝 3. Expected Behavior

Contributors should:

- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Assume good faith while still maintaining healthy skepticism.
- Welcome corrections and offer them respectfully.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.
- Follow the technical rules in `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `LICENSE.md`.
- Help maintain a collaborative, inclusive environment.

---

## 📣 4. Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:

- Open an issue if you feel comfortable.
- If not, contact the maintainers through GitHub’s private channels.

Reports will be handled discreetly and respectfully.

---

## 🔧 5. Enforcement

Project maintainers may:

- Issue warnings  
- Temporarily restrict participation  
- Permanently ban contributors who repeatedly violate the Code  

These actions are taken to protect the community and uphold the humanitarian
invariant that defines the project.

---

## 🌍 6. Commitment to a Safe Research Space

Lean‑Hallucination‑Guard is a place for:

- geometric reasoning  
- interpretability research  
- safe experimentation  
- collaborative learning  

This Code of Conduct ensures that the human side of the project remains as
carefully designed as the technical side.

Thank you for helping maintain a respectful, safe, and principled environment.
